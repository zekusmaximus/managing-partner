# Product Context: Managing Partner

## Experience

A player becomes the managing partner of a fictional Washington, D.C. government relations firm. The dashboard shows current financial and organizational health. Finances, HR, Clients, and Inbox let the player inspect details and make decisions. Advance Month applies recurring costs, collections, client and team changes, then presents new alerts and scenarios.

Staff capacity now sets client service coverage: effective lobbyists and attorneys supply slots, support staff improve their throughput, and overload weakens satisfaction and renewals. Players gain clients and answer client requests through the inbox. Manual overdue collections are available once per game month. The phone dashboard foregrounds cash, coverage, and alerts; phone finance reports use a selector and compact P&L period view.

Early player notes identified warnings with no obvious response and unclear action tradeoffs. Warnings now link to the relevant decision view. One paid client meeting per month can improve a selected at-risk client's satisfaction and recover part of that client's overdue invoices. Action previews show the modeled benefit, cost, and uncertainty, including that writing off debt yields no cash and lowers profit.

A 34-step tutorial introduces the metrics and actions. Contextual help and a glossary explain concepts throughout the app. Progress resumes in the same browser, and New Game resets the simulation and tutorial together. Receivable balances are shown by actual client where known; former-client and unassigned balances remain visible. Resolved inbox messages show the choice and its actual result.

## UX Goals

- Make the current month, cash position, and next action easy to find.
- Keep all five routes usable on phone and desktop widths.
- Make navigation, decisions, help, and tutorial actions work by keyboard.
- Give immediate, truthful feedback about the consequences of decisions.
- Keep the simplified financial model internally consistent and explain its limits honestly.

## First-Pass Boundaries

One local save slot is supported. Accounts, cloud sync, new win/loss conditions, and a full real-world accounting model are later product decisions. The game tracks an estimated tax balance and fictional late charge; its month-opening estimate does not recalculate after later actions. A 3–5 person new-player session plan is ready; observations of the revised service loop, mobile navigation, and tutorial remain pending before deeper tax or persistence work.
