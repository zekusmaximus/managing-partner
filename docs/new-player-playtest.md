# New-player playtest: service, retention, cash, and navigation

## Goal and participants

Observe 3–5 people who have not used Managing Partner. Run individual 25–30 minute sessions; include at least two phone-width sessions. These are qualitative usability sessions, not a statistically representative balance study. Start each participant with a fresh game and record the app commit, device width, and whether they chose the tutorial. Do not explain the new service or collections rules before the tasks.

Use the same build for every participant. Before each session, choose **New Game** and confirm the reset; this clears the one browser-local save and tutorial progress. If participants use separate devices, start each device with a fresh game. Share anonymized notes from the table below after the sessions for analysis and the next iteration.

## Facilitator script

1. **First impression (3 minutes).** Ask: “What is your role, what is the firm’s current position, and what would you inspect before advancing?” Note which dashboard metrics and alerts they notice first.
2. **Service and retention (8 minutes).** Ask them to inspect HR and Clients, describe how staffing might affect service and renewals, make one staffing choice, then advance several months. Before each advance ask what they expect to happen. Have them locate the result and explain why it happened. Do not suggest hiring or firing.
3. **Cash decisions (5 minutes).** Ask them to find overdue receivables and use the manual collections action. Ask whether they can use it again in the same month and where they learned the rule. Have them compare billed revenue, collections, and cash.
4. **Inbox decision (5 minutes).** Ask them to handle a pending client or business opportunity if one appears, then find the recorded outcome. If none appears, record that and continue rather than manufacturing one.
5. **Phone finances (4 minutes, phone sessions).** Ask them to find Accounts Receivable and the P&L year-to-date profit without pointing to the tabs or table scroll area.
6. **Wrap-up (3 minutes).** Ask: “What drove client satisfaction and renewals? Which choice felt least fair or least clear? What would you do next month?”

The facilitator may pause or stop if the participant is stuck, but should record the point and prompt given. Avoid teaching the interface during a task.

For each task, mark **completed unaided**, **completed with a prompt**, or **not completed**; record time and a short quote or observation. Capture the game month, active clients, billable and support staff, service coverage, cash, and credit drawn at the end. Recordings are optional and should only be made with participant consent.

## Review after sessions

Group observations by repeated problems, then prioritize any issue that blocks a decision or causes players to form a false causal model. Check whether participants understand that staffing affects service and renewal, can find the once-per-month collections limit, and can reach the relevant mobile finance data. Re-run the seeded balance report after rule changes, but keep those scripted results separate from participant observations. Decide on deeper tax accounting or save portability only after reviewing these sessions.
